# orca-keel-landing project brief

The approved outcome, constraints and permissions for anyone working on the Orca Keel landing page, human or agent.

## Outcome

- A public landing page for Orca Keel, the household computer, that says what it is in the family's words, gives the installer the trust story one layer down, and says plainly that it is in development. It goes up before the product exists, while the prototype is built.
- The page is built from the Orca brand kit (`brand/kit/`): the Signal mark as the seal, the Harbor palette, Instrument Sans and Newsreader, and the voice sheet.
- Later, when the maintainer decides: a domain, a way to be told when the first run ships, and the price.

## Constraints

- Static files only. No server, no build, no scripts, no forms, no analytics ([docs/DEVOPS.md](docs/DEVOPS.md)).
- Voice rules from [brand/kit/voice-sheet.md](brand/kit/voice-sheet.md): plain words, the specific thing named, limits stated as capabilities are, no exclamation marks.
- No prices, dates, unit counts or contact address until the maintainer gives them. Bracketed placeholders stay visible rather than being guessed.
- The page never links to a live product or exposes anything running in the maintainer's house.
- Proprietary licence for the source and content ([LICENSE](LICENSE)); the names and the mark are the studio's ([NOTICE](NOTICE)). The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.

## Permissions

- Jason merges pull requests, connects GitHub Pages and manages the domain.
- Agents work on branches and open draft pull requests. They never merge, deploy, buy or change a domain.
- Posting to social accounts, sending outreach and spending money need Jason's explicit say each time.
- No standing exceptions are recorded. The initial history and these documents were pushed to `main` directly when the repository was created, at Jason's request on 2026-10-21.
